Who we are

About the Institute

A Gurukul is not a slogan here — it's the timetable. Mornings begin with assembly and reflection; afternoons belong to compilers, spreadsheets and studio work. Shri Swaminarayan Gurukul Institute of Information Technology sits within the larger Shaikshanik Sankul campus at Chhaya, Porbandar. We run undergraduate programs in computer applications and information technology inside a residential-style campus that still keeps its festivals, its assemblies and its sense of community intact. Faculty mentors track every student individually, batch sizes stay small by design, and the technical curriculum sits alongside cultural, sporting and leadership programming — because a computer science graduate who has organised a 200-person festival is a different kind of employee than one who hasn't.
